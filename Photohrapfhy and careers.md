# Photography and Careers

Photography combines visual storytelling, technical skill, and creative judgment. Photographers use images to document events, communicate ideas, promote products, and express personal perspectives.

## Career paths

- **Portrait photography:** Photographing individuals, families, and professional headshots.
- **Wedding and event photography:** Capturing celebrations, conferences, and live events.
- **Commercial photography:** Creating images for advertising, products, food, fashion, and brands.
- **Photojournalism and documentary photography:** Telling factual stories about people, places, and events.
- **Fine art photography:** Developing personal projects for exhibitions, publications, and sales.
- **Scientific and technical photography:** Producing precise images for research, medicine, industry, or documentation.

## Core skills

Photographers need an understanding of light, composition, exposure, and editing. Communication, organization, and reliability are also essential when working with clients and collaborators.

Freelance work adds business responsibilities such as pricing, budgeting, contracts, marketing, and managing image files.

## Building experience

Practice with focused projects, learn to evaluate your own images, and assemble a portfolio that reflects the work you want to do. Assisting experienced photographers and collaborating on small projects can provide practical experience.

## Working life

Photography careers can involve freelance assignments, staff positions, or a combination of photography and related work. Schedules and income vary by specialty and employment arrangement. A sustainable career requires both strong images and consistent professional service.
